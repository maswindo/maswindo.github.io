💸 Vendor Spend & Invoice Tracker

Status: In Progress

This tool analyzes all vendor invoices across a 12-month period and generates actionable insights for spending, material costs, and warehouse usage. It combines Excel-based automation with visual dashboards to track patterns and help inform purchasing decisions.

📊 What I Built

  • Invoice parser that logs line-items from raw vendor documents
  • Pivot tables tracking spend by vendor, warehouse, and month
  • Dynamic cost-per-item tracking over time
  • Top vendors, most used materials, and monthly spend breakdowns
  • Visualization-ready structure for Tableau dashboards

🧰 Tools & Tech Stack

  • Excel (VBA + formula logic)
  • Power Query (for cleaning/transforming vendor data)
  • Tableau (for visualizing vendor performance and spend trends)
